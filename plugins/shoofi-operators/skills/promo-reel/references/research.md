# What the best Reels and Stories ads do (researched 2026-11-06)

The styles are built on these. Numbers are Meta's own Reels research (likelihood of ranking in the
top 20% for purchase intent / brand interest), reported by Social Media Today; food findings are
TikTok's study of 200 top food & drink videos.

| Rule | Evidence | How the styles apply it |
|---|---|---|
| Show the product more than once | Meta 2.7× | every style returns to the food ≥ 2 times (the end card's store logo counts as brand, not product) |
| Context: why this, why now | Meta 5.3× | one context line: the terms, what's in the dish, "to your door" |
| Brand + message in the first 5 s | Meta 1.7× | the Shoofi pill from second 0; the offer or hook by second 2 |
| Brand ≤ 25% of the duration | Meta 4.8× | logo + end card ≤ 3 s of 12 |
| Speech and music together | Meta 2.1× | Explore voice (Eleven v4) over a catalog music bed |
| A CTA, said and shown | Meta 1.9× | «اطلب من شوفي» on the end card and in the voice |
| Hook with picture and sound | Meta 1.5× | a food sound (sizzle, pour, crunch — search_sfx) on the first cut |
| Native elements (emoji) | Meta 2.5× | **capped at one**: our measured voice says customers rarely use emoji (2.2% of 3,166 messages) |
| The product at the centre | TikTok: 7 in 10 | the dish fills the frame; textures, steam, sauce |
| Product sounds | TikTok: 64% enjoy them | see the hook row |
| Look different from friends' stories | Deliveroo's story ads: brand colours, high-contrast type, fast movement | brand palette, cuts every 1–2.5 s, one fast thing per shot |
| Words readable muted, inside the safe zone | AdEspresso / Karola Karlson examples; IG UI covers top bar, caption area, right rail | y 260–1440, x 80–860 |

Live examples to browse: Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library — Uber Eats, Deliveroo, talabat,
Wolt, McDonald's), TikTok Creative Center top ads (Food & Beverage). Copy the shape, never the ad.
