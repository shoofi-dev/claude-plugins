# Social Marketing — FIELD (what the others post)

Owner, 2026-11-20: *"scan haat.delivery and wolt.arabic, watch them once a day, one view of the
topics they post about so we can cover them too; scan the big delivery brands; I want my agents
to be the best in the industry."*

**Evidence grades used here** (in addition to PLAYBOOK.md's):
- **[SCAN]** — counted by us on 2026-11-20 from the accounts' own posts (likes, comments, video
  views, dates, captions). What a scan cannot see: Stories, shares, saves, who was tagged, and
  **whether a post was boosted with money** — a competitor's views may be bought. Likes and
  comments are harder to buy, so judge their posts by those.
- **[WATCH]** — today's state from the daily watch (§6). Prefer it over the numbers below once
  it has run for a few weeks; the numbers below are a snapshot.

## 2. Wolt Arabic (`wolt.arabic`) — money without a voice [SCAN]

6,225 followers, 102 posts. 87 posts read (2025-09-18 → 2026-09-06).

- **Silent since 2026-09-06** — four posts in the last 90 days. An open field on the days they
  are quiet. (Check [WATCH] before saying this; they may return.)
- A usual post: **6 likes, 0 comments**, while its Reels show 30,000–300,000 views. That gap is
  what bought reach looks like (inference — the scan cannot see ad spend).
- Almost every post is an offer or a slogan: free delivery on Fridays, "free delivery all
  summer", 15–25% in set hours, "leave it to Wolt". Graphics and stock scenes, no local people.
- The only posts with comments are "first 10 correct answers win a ₪50 coupon" (20–39 comments).
- Their single best post is their single human one: a walk with a courier who plays football
  and does cupping therapy — 92 likes (2026-09-06).
- Town launches are done with a local radio clip (Kafr Qara, July 2026) and a name-check of the
  towns ("people of Tayibe, Tira and Qalansawe").

**The lesson of the pair:** Haat's stories about real people beat Haat's own offers ten to one,
and Wolt's offers with no people get nothing at any budget. In our towns the people are the
content.

What follows from it:
1. **Volume does not buy engagement.** Jahez posts nine times a week for 9 likes; HungerStation
   seven Reels a week for 22. Zomato posts twice a week and gets the most per post. Four good
   posts a week (decision 2) is not a handicap.
2. **Carousels and one-line pictures out-earn Reels in likes for the two best accounts.**
   Swiggy: carousels 8,143 against Reels 549. Zomato: pictures 4,423 and carousels 3,453 against
   Reels 1,277. Reels still bring the views (reach to non-followers, PLAYBOOK §2a). The mix
   wants both — and **we cannot publish a carousel yet** (PLAYBOOK §9b).
3. **Captions are one short line.** 32–90 characters at the top accounts. The idea is in the
   picture or the video; the caption is the punchline. Hashtags: none (Talabat alone uses 2–3).
4. **The posts that travel ask the viewer to do one small thing with a friend**: Swiggy's "tag
   your school bestie" (80,429 likes), "which will you eat?" over a grid of dishes (84,698;
   245,332 for the festival-sweets version), "send this to your partner" (39,118).
5. **A quiz about a shared past fills the comments**: HungerStation's "who really lived the
   nineties?" posts got 26,000–58,000 comments each against a usual 12 — a draw for a voucher,
   answer in the comments with a hashtag.
6. **The courier as a character**: Talabat's best post of the period is a rider edit — 34,399
   likes against a usual 79. Its riders appear in its festival greetings too.
7. **Everyone rides the same moment the same week.** GTA 6's release: DoorDash, Deliveroo,
   Zomato, Swiggy, Talabat **and Haat** all posted between 13 and 19 November 2026. We did not.
   The Microsoft outage: three of them, within a day.
8. **Restaurants behind the scenes earn comments** for Uber Eats ("…is officially a Texas food
   legend", 407 comments against a usual 167): a real kitchen, a named place.
9. DoorDash's World Cup series with a football star reached 3.3M likes — budget, not method.
   It does not transfer.

## 5. What we do about it — rules

Each is a candidate until our own numbers confirm it (PLAYBOOK §13).

1. **People before offers.** One slot a week is a real person of ours: a store owner's story, a
   courier, a customer's town. Their own words in the caption's first line, the town named.
   It needs real footage or a real photo, so the plan says exactly what to film and the owner
   uploads it. [SCAN §1–2] · study rules 5, 21
2. **Never copy, never name.** A competitor's post is a signal about the *occasion*, not a
   template. We answer the occasion in our voice, with our stores, and never mention another
   app — not as a joke, not in a hashtag. A campaign of theirs is not a reason for a discount
   of ours: coupons are the owner's (CORE.md, the coupon rule).
3. **A moment needs a twist and a deadline.** Ride a moment when (a) the watch shows three or
   more accounts on it or one competitor, (b) it is ours to ride (`local`), (c) we have something
   to add — Shadi, a town, a dish — and (d) it can go out within 48 hours. Otherwise drop it;
   a late joke is worse than none. Keep one of the four weekly slots loose for this.
   [SCAN §4.7] · study rules 14, 16
4. **One launch post, one last-day post.** No reminder posts in between; they are the weakest
   thing Haat publishes. Put reminders in Stories. [SCAN §1]
5. **A greeting must carry something** — a question, a memory, a courier, an act of giving. A
   bare "كل عام وأنتم بخير" card is the second-weakest thing both competitors publish.
   Ramadan is for giving, couriers and safety; humour waits for Eid and ordinary days.
   [SCAN §1–2] · study rules 9, 10
6. **Every post has a caption**, one or two short lines in dialect, the point first. Ours have
   had none. [SCAN §3, §4.3]
7. **One "do this with a friend" post a fortnight**: tag the friend who…, which of these four,
   finish the sentence. Real menu photos in the grid, never a drawn dish. [SCAN §4.4]
8. **Comments are earned with a small ask and a small prize.** A comment-to-answer post only
   when an approved coupon campaign exists to pay the prize, at most twice a month, and never
   "follow and tag three friends" (PLAYBOOK §8b). A nostalgia question works without a prize.
   [SCAN §1, §4.5]
9. **Order data is a post.** On a world food day or at month end: "the most ordered … in
   <town>" from our own orders. A number, a real photo of the winning dish, no customer named.
   As a carousel when we can publish one. [SCAN §1 May/June] · study rules 7, 8
10. **The forward test.** Before a slot is handed in: would the owner forward this to the family
    group? If not, it is a notice — move it to a Story or drop it. · study rule 2
11. **At most two of the four weekly posts are a dish or an offer.** · study rule 1 (Zomato ~8%
    food, Swiggy ~32%)
12. **A written "never" list**: religion as a joke, politics, security events, identity, other
    brands, a courier's speed or pay. A festival joke gets a second read. · study rule 20
13. **What does not transfer**: celebrity tie-ins, daily "admin" banter, banter with famous
    brands, war-room speed, edgy jokes, AI-drawn food. (study, "What does NOT transfer")

## 6. The daily watch — where to read today's state

`shoofi-server` reads the watched accounts once a day at 06:40 (docs/social-watch.md):
`shoofi.socialWatchAccounts` (group `competitor` / `inspiration` / `own`, followers by day) and
`shoofi.socialWatchPosts` (date, kind, caption, likes, comments, views, and a model's reading:
`topic`, `event` — a slug shared by every brand's post about the same thing — `local`, and a
one-line Hebrew `gist`). The owner's screen is מרכז שיווק → «מה מפרסמים».

- The weekly plan's job text already carries the last two weeks of it. Use it under rules 2–3.
- An event is "covered" only when our own account **published** a post the model gave the same
  slug. The model sometimes names one event two ways; read the list, do not trust the flag blind.
- `topic` and `gist` come from the caption alone. The model did not see the video.
- When a brief or a plan cites the watch, give the account, the date and the numbers — and say
  that views may be paid.

## 7. Thin spots

- One day's scan. Likes move for days after a post; the newest posts in every table are
  under-counted.
- No Stories, shares or saves for anyone but us — so "what earns shares" (the number PLAYBOOK §1
  judges by) is **not** answered by this file; likes and comments are a stand-in.
- Tira Eat has no business Instagram account that Meta's reader could find under the obvious
  names; it is not watched.
- Facebook pages and TikTok were not scanned.
- Talabat's per-country accounts and Jahez's tone were not studied.
