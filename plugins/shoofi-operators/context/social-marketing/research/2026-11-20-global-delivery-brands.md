# What the best food-delivery brands do on social media — study (20 Nov 2026)

## How to read this

- Every concrete claim ends in the URL it was read at. **(snippet)** means it was seen only in a search-result summary, not on the fetched page; treat those as weaker.
- **Inference** is marked as such. Numbers carry their year. Numbers from award entries and press releases are self-reported by the brand or agency.
- Follower and engagement figures from Qoruz, HypeAuditor and AltIndex are third-party estimates, not brand-confirmed.
- **What could not be verified for any brand:** posting cadence, format mix (Reels / carousels / images / Stories), typical Reel length, and detailed caption habits (emoji, hashtags). Instagram is login-walled and no article or benchmark report gave these. They are left out rather than guessed. No Socialinsider or Rival IQ data on any of these brands was found.
- Expected items that could not be verified were dropped; they are listed per brand under "Not verified".

## Step 2 — Brand by brand

## 1. Swiggy and Swiggy Instamart (India)

**Accounts and scale**
- @swiggyindia: 1.5M followers, 2.36% engagement rate, 34.5K average likes, 306 comments, 1.6M average Reel views (Qoruz, as of Nov 2026). That is higher engagement and double the Reel views of Zomato on a smaller base. https://qoruz.com/swiggy_in/instagram
- Instamart runs a separate account, now @instamart (May 2025). https://www.freepressjournal.in/viral/rooh-afza-with-samosa-idli-soaked-in-rasmalai-syrup-swiggy-instamart-shares-video-of-bizarre-dishes-while-posing-as-food-vlogger
- Instamart Reels engagement 6.90% vs Zepto 5.53% and Blinkit 4.91% (Qoruz via trade press, about 2025, snippet). https://www.pitchonnet.com/pitch-feature/how-influencers-are-delivering-results-for-indias-quick-comm-giants-36246.html
- Bio: "khaana khau raat bhar, crazy collabs karu har baat par". https://www.mediainfoline.com/brand/how-swiggy-is-redefining-brand-collabs-in-meme-culture

**Content pillars**
- Qoruz category mix for @swiggyindia (2026): Food 31.58%, Arts & Entertainment 31.57%, Sports 15.79%, Beauty 10.53%, Gaming 10.53%. Roughly one post in three is about food. https://qoruz.com/swiggy_in/instagram
- 2019 buckets: memes, topicals (cricket, news), polished food photos, puns, weekly quizzes (#SwiggyTuesday, #LightningFastQuiz), emotional films. https://www.socialsamosa.com/2019/02/swiggy-social-media-strategy
- Since 2024: collabs with whoever is the meme of the week, plus a running "admin" persona and cat-fan storyline. https://winners.kyoorius.com/winners/2026/kca/12320-give-swiggy-admin-a-raise

**Posts and campaigns that worked**
1. **Voice of Hunger (Feb–Mar 2019).** Five daily challenges: draw a food shape (kebab skewer, shawarma, fish, nacho, 21-stack pancake) with the waveform of an Instagram DM voice note; prize a year of vouchers. 1.5 lakh+ (150,000+) entries in 10 days, 50+ DMs a minute, +30K followers. https://socialsamosa.com/2019/04/swiggy-instagram-case-study-voice-of-hunger
2. **Why is this a Swiggy ad? (Diwali 2022).** One cryptic, clue-filled image on social, print and billboards; ₹1 lakh for the best fan theory; an 18-minute reveal video (snippet). Two bronze Lions in 2023 (snippet). https://www.storyboard18.com/quantum-brief/why-did-swiggy-make-a-cryptic-ad-inviting-whacky-user-generated-theories-2778.htm · https://www.socialsamosa.com/cannes-lions/talented-cannes-lions-success-4761408
3. **Give Swiggy Admin a Raise (2024–26).** The account speaks as an overworked "admin". Self-reported over one year: 897.8M+ views, 661M+ reach, 22M+ shares, 59.6M+ engagements, no paid ads. https://winners.kyoorius.com/winners/2026/kca/12320-give-swiggy-admin-a-raise
4. **Meme-of-the-week collabs (2024–25).** The creator famous for mispronouncing "croissant", "Tedhe Medhe Guy", cricketer lookalikes during IPL 2025; "millions of organic views". https://www.mediainfoline.com/brand/how-swiggy-is-redefining-brand-collabs-in-meme-culture
5. **Instamart food-vlogger parody (May 2025).** A Reel of absurd pairings (samosa with Rooh Afza, Maggi in Coke), caption "that one food vlogger reel on your feed." Coca-Cola India commented "Admin yaar, who hurt you?" https://www.freepressjournal.in/viral/rooh-afza-with-samosa-idli-soaked-in-rasmalai-syrup-swiggy-instamart-shares-video-of-bizarre-dishes-while-posing-as-food-vlogger
6. **Instamart odd-order posts, Cricket World Cup 2023.** "Someone from thane just ordered 51 nariyals... if it's for finals, the world cup is coming home for real." (snippet). https://www.freepressjournal.in/viral/viral-news-thane-man-who-ordered-240-agarbattis-during-ind-vs-nz-semi-finals-brings-home-51-coconuts-during-cwc-2023-final
7. **How India Swiggy'd 2024.** Year-end data report: 83M biryanis, "158 a minute, about 2 a second" (snippet). https://www.swiggy.com/corporate/press-release/how-india-swiggyd-its-way-through-2024-a-deep-dive-into-the-nations-eatopia/
8. **#KhaliKaKhaaliCart (Instamart, 2022).** A fill-in-the-blank sent by DM; engagement +125%, followers +12% in 4 days (snippet). https://business-standard.com/content/press-releases-ani/swiggyinstamart-innovates-with-the-khalikakhaalicart-campaign-boosting-its-social-engagement-by-over-125-per-cent-during-its-birthday-week-122092000003_1.html
9. **AI cat chef (Nov 2024).** AI images of a cat cooking each city's street food; 30,000+ likes in days (snippet). Listed for completeness — it breaks a "no AI food" rule. https://www.freepressjournal.in/ampstories/photo-gallery/viral-swiggy-shares-ai-generated-pics-of-cat-preparing-mouth-watering-street-food-of-mumbai-bangalore-delhi-more

**Voice**
- "We take our work seriously, but we don't take ourselves seriously." Swiggy is "unapologetically Gen Z"; Instamart slightly older and more mainstream (VP Brand, June 2025). https://www.socialsamosa.com/sm-live/mayur-hola-building-brand-9449676
- "Realness beats reelness." / "Participating in trends is not enough, we're here to start them." (July 2025). https://indiantelevision.com/news-headline/swiggy-serves-up-lols-as-it-swaps-menus-for-memes-on-the-gram/
- Speed rule: if they cannot react within 24–48 hours, they skip the trend (2021). https://www.afaqs.com/news/profile/meet-shikha-gupta-the-creative-mind-behind-swiggys-funky-puns-emailers-social-content

**Restaurants, creators, couriers, UGC, giveaways, data**
- Creators: deliberately small, non-metro, meme-famous ones. https://indiantelevision.com/news-headline/swiggy-serves-up-lols-as-it-swaps-menus-for-memes-on-the-gram/
- Couriers: the "What's in a Name" film came from research that riders felt uneasy about how customers addressed them. https://www.afaqs.com/news/profile/meet-shikha-gupta-the-creative-mind-behind-swiggys-funky-puns-emailers-social-content
- Contests are the participation engine (voice notes, fan theories, DM fill-ins) — sources above.

**Occasions**
- New Year's Eve live order ticker ("90,000 burgers by 9:30 pm") (2025, snippet). https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fiphones-to-biryanis-swiggy-runs-live-updates-of-massive-orders-as-2025-comes-to-a-close-3848264
- IPL: 60% off within 6 minutes of every six (2025, snippet). https://www.campaignindia.in/article/swiggy-links-ipl-sixes-with-real-time-snapchat-ads/4f3kcec1ehb1w6y1zw9cq3tnmp
- Ramadan: a Hyderabad order report — 1M biryanis, 400K haleem (2024, snippet). https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/india-1-million-biryanis-400000-haleem-orders-on-swiggy-this-ramadan-in-hyderabad-1.95268816
- Also Diwali, Valentine's Day (planned ahead), monsoon safety. https://www.socialsamosa.com/2019/02/swiggy-social-media-strategy

**What it does not do, and failures**
- Not salesy: "We are fine as long as the idea helps us be your friend." https://www.afaqs.com/marketing/inside-the-swiggy-universe-with-mayur-hola-9587034
- Dropped humour in early lockdown. https://www.afaqs.com/news/profile/meet-shikha-gupta-the-creative-mind-behind-swiggys-funky-puns-emailers-social-content
- Admits "Sometimes it does feel like we're trying too hard to fit in." https://www.socialsamosa.com/sm-live/mayur-hola-building-brand-9449676
- **Mar 2023:** Instamart Holi egg billboard; a boycott hashtag trended; removed with no statement (snippet). https://www.exchange4media.com/marketing-news/swiggy-whipped-over-holi-eggs-ad-takes-it-down-after-boycott-calls-125764.html
- **Nov 2025:** Instamart reportedly commented "brocode" on a Reel of a man borrowing a rider's T-shirt to enter a building; criticised as a security risk. https://www.freepressjournal.in/viral/video-man-trades-t-shirt-with-swiggy-instamart-delivery-partner-to-surprise-girlfriend-swiggy-calls-it-brocode-netizens-flag-security-risk

**Team**
- No mainline agency; two social agencies on retainer; most work in-house. The VP Brand reviews social content and says he sees "maybe 85%" of output (July 2025). https://www.afaqs.com/marketing/inside-the-swiggy-universe-with-mayur-hola-9587034

**Not verified:** "Swiggy it" as a campaign line; exact Cannes tally for Voice of Hunger (sources disagree: 3 bronze vs 1 silver + 2 bronze).

## 2. Zomato (India)

**Accounts and scale**
- @zomato: 2.1M followers, 1.19% engagement rate, 24.9K average likes, 298 comments, 807K average Reel views (Qoruz, as of Nov 2026). Bio: "professional door bell ringer :)". https://qoruz.com/fasefoodie/instagram
- Growth points: 891K (Nov 2023), 994K (July 2024) (snippets from a marketing newsletter). https://growthmatrix.beehiiv.com/p/swiggy-vs-zomato-ultimate-marketing-faceoff

**Content pillars**
- Qoruz category mix (2026): Arts & Entertainment 50%, Sports 25%, Food 8.33%, Finance 8.33%, Gaming 8.33%. Food is a small minority of what it posts. https://qoruz.com/fasefoodie/instagram
- Social Samosa's 2019 reading of its Twitter (the article's own grouping): reverse-psychology posts, trend-jacking, social topics, banter with other brands, food symbolism. https://www.socialsamosa.com/2019/07/zomato-twitter-strategy-and-quick-takeaways

**Posts and campaigns that worked**
1. **"Ghar ka khana" (3 July 2019, X).** "Guys, kabhi kabhi ghar ka khana bhi kha lena chahiye" (sometimes you should eat home food too) — a delivery app telling people not to order. The founder replied publicly; other brands copied the format; Zomato closed with "think of your own good tweets". https://www.socialsamosa.com/2019/07/zomato-twitter-strategy-and-quick-takeaways
2. **"Ankita from Bhopal" (2 Aug 2023, X).** "Ankita from Bhopal please stop sending food to your ex on cash on delivery. This is the 3rd time – he is refusing to pay!" https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/trending/zomato-asks-ankita-to-stop-sending-food-to-her-ex-on-cash-on-delivery-as-he-was-not-paying-netizens-having-field-day-531425 — reported at 11,000+ likes and 1,000+ retweets (snippet). https://etvbharat.com/english/state/madhya-pradesh/zomato-asks-bhopal-girl-to-stop-sending-food-to-ex-on-cod-netizens-cant-stop-laughing/na20230803124509372372085
3. **Zomato x Blinkit billboards (Jan 2023).** Two adjacent boards punning on a film line ("ask for milk, we'll give milk" / "ask for kheer, we'll give kheer"). Netflix, Pizza Hut and others copied the template within days (snippet). https://www.storyboard18.com/quantum-brief/a-zomato-and-blinkit-collaboration-on-billboards-doodh-mangoge-4658.htm
4. **Push notifications as shareable content.** Users screenshot and post them ("I download the zomato app just to read its hilarious notification", 2022). https://www.socialsamosa.com/2022/11/new-age-brands-redefined-age-old-push-notifications
5. **Year-end data threads.** 2020 as a meme thread (top customer: 1,380 orders); 2024: 9+ crore (90M+) biryanis and a ₹5 lakh single restaurant bill (snippets). https://www.business-standard.com/amp/lifestyle/zomato-2024-year-end-report-foodie-spent-rs-5-lakh-in-a-single-restaurant-nc-124122700762_1.html
6. **18th birthday ad (10 July 2026).** A full red newspaper page with no logo: "happy 18th birthday to us. thank you for keeping us in your hearts (and bank statements)." / "no logo required you know who we are :)". https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/trends/story/you-know-who-we-are-this-companys-unnamed-18th-birthday-ad-is-a-masterclass-on-brand-recall-542215-2026-07-10
7. **Creator in uniform (2021).** Comedian Danish Sait's delivery-shift vlog, 53K+ views in 4 days (snippet). https://www.socialsamosa.com/2021/08/danish-sait-signature-zomato-uniform-campaign

**Voice**
- Casual mixed Hindi-English, lowercase, short, with emoji; often does not mention the service at all. https://www.socialsamosa.com/2019/07/zomato-twitter-strategy-and-quick-takeaways
- Speed: a push notification went out "an hour or two" after a news event (the PUBG ban). https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/the-ingredients-fueling-zomatos-marketing-juggernaut

**Restaurants, creators, couriers, data**
- Couriers are a recurring hero: a film teaching the sign-language "thank you" for deaf delivery partners (snippet). https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/zomatos-new-ad-teaches-users-to-sign-thank-you-to-deaf-delivery-partners-10871707
- Restaurant-led content and giveaways: nothing verified.

**Occasions**
- Verified: cricket matches, Raksha Bandhan, World Environment Day, year-end, its own birthday. https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/brand-marketing/zomatos-advertising-playbook-a-look-at-heartwarming-campaigns-74624.htm

**What it does not do, and failures**
- No agencies: "We've paused working with agencies for quite a long time. We write our own scripts, our own creatives." No AI in marketing creative. Paid media only behind content it is confident in (head of marketing, Jan 2024). https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/the-ingredients-fueling-zomatos-marketing-juggernaut
- Jan 2026: moved away from dish-specific push nudges after the founder called earlier tactics "borderline manipulative". https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/zomato-revises-notification-strategy-to-avoid-prompting-unhealthy-eating-10978803
- **Aug 2021:** celebrity "Every customer is a star" ads called tone-deaf on courier conditions. https://sg.news.yahoo.com/zomato-indian-food-delivery-app-143324131.html
- **Mar 2022:** 10-minute delivery announcement; rider-safety backlash within a day (snippet). https://theprint.in/economy/zomato-faces-backlash-on-social-media-over-10-min-delivery-plan/884064
- **Aug 2022:** "Mahakal thali" ad; temple priests objected; withdrawn with an apology (snippet). https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/nation/amid-backlash-zomato-withdraws-mahakal-advt-featuring-hrithik-roshan-expresses-apology-423922

## 3. DoorDash (USA)

**Accounts and scale**
- Instagram @doordash about 489K and TikTok about 487K (Jul 2026, AltIndex, snippet). HypeAuditor shows 614K, undated — the figures conflict. https://altindex.com/news/doordash-down-social-media-followers-up
- For a company its size this is a small following: its results come from individual posts travelling, not from a huge base (inference).

**Content pillars and series**
- No source gives shares by pillar. The head of social keeps 70% of the calendar for the team's own plan and 30% for requests from other departments. https://runneth.com/library/talk/zaria-parvez-duolingo-doordash-why-paid-organic-social-have-to-work-together/
- Recurring series: DoorDash DMs celebrities asking "what did you last buy on DoorDash" (one answer: a veil, two hours before a wedding). https://www.itiger.com/news/2617545423
- Entertainment first, brand second. https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/03/06/doordash-duolingo-zaria-parvez-hire/

**Posts and campaigns that worked**
1. **"DoorDash-All-The-Ads" (Super Bowl 2024).** An absurdly long promo code revealed in the spot; one winner got an item from every advertiser (about $500K, including a BMW and a 30-lb bucket of mayonnaise). A disguised demonstration of "we deliver more than food". Cannes Titanium Grand Prix. https://www.adweek.com/creativity/doordashs-epic-super-bowl-giveaway-just-won-the-cannes-lions-titanium-grand-prix/
2. **"Bouquets for Living Men" (2024).** Cannes Bronze, Social & Creator. https://lovethework.com/work-awards/entries/711793
3. **"Your Door to Mom's Heart".** Surprise reunions plus creators interviewing their mums; 42 assets, 36M impressions, 400K flower deliveries (self-reported). The team also sent flowers and gift cards to individual commenters found through social listening. https://shortyawards.com/17th/your-door-to-moms-heart-campaign-for-doordash-dept
4. **TV-finale debrief (2025).** One 400K-follower creator and her mum talked about a series finale for 20 minutes; 2M views in 48 hours and 15.93% engagement (self-reported). https://shortyawards.com/18th/doordash-x-the-summer-i-turned-pretty-delivering-a-viral-finale
5. **Rob Rausch TikTok (2026).** A reality star handling a rat snake while talking about "petty beef"; nearly 11M views. https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/03/06/doordash-duolingo-zaria-parvez-hire/
6. **Ziwe "Bad Boyfriend Bootcamp" (Valentine's 2026).** The idea came from TikTok comments about bad gift-givers. Under 3,000 YouTube views — it earned trade coverage but not an audience. https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/03/06/doordash-duolingo-zaria-parvez-hire/
7. **Back to School "BTS scaries".** Skits and flowcharts where every path ends at DoorDash; 379M impressions (self-reported, mostly paid). https://shortyawards.com/16th/back-to-school-campaign-for-doordash-by-dept
8. **Reactive stunts.** Free brooms for basketball fans went from idea to billboards in about 48 hours; a follow-up took about 24. https://runneth.com/library/talk/zaria-parvez-duolingo-doordash-why-paid-organic-social-have-to-work-together/

**Voice**
- Team mantra: "lean in to the meme". Commenters are about 5% of engagement but drive the narrative; comments are mined for ideas. https://www.itiger.com/news/2617545423
- The test for a post: "good enough to share in a group chat if you didn't work at DoorDash". https://runneth.com/library/talk/zaria-parvez-duolingo-doordash-why-paid-organic-social-have-to-work-together/

**Merchants, creators, couriers, UGC, giveaways, data**
- 5.6K creators, 17.5K posts, 2.8B impressions (May 2023–Apr 2024). https://www.creatoriq.com/blog/doordash-influencer-marketing-social-media
- Each talent deal yields 6 to 7 extra pieces of content. https://www.itiger.com/news/2617545423
- Data stories: an annual Delivery Trends Report plus Thanksgiving and Game Day order reports (snippet). https://about.doordash.com/en-us/news/doordash-delivery-trends-report
- Couriers as content: not verified.

**Occasions**
- Super Bowl, Valentine's, Mother's Day, back to school, a summer membership month, Thanksgiving, sports finals. https://www.marketingdive.com/news/doordash-delivers-memes-reality-tv-moms-for-mothers-day-campaign/819175/
- Leadership plans 3 to 4 reactive moments per quarter — not daily trend-chasing. https://runneth.com/library/talk/zaria-parvez-duolingo-doordash-why-paid-organic-social-have-to-work-together/

**What it does not do, and failures**
- Social is not the place for cut-downs of TV ads: "social wasn't the distribution channel, it was the insight base". https://www.itiger.com/news/2617545423
- A stated risk rule: 10–15% negative sentiment is acceptable, 30–50% is not; past the line they delete the post and learn. https://www.itiger.com/news/2617545423
- Not chasing immediate sales: "We're not looking for you to move the needle yet". https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/03/06/doordash-duolingo-zaria-parvez-hire/
- **Mar 2025:** the Klarna "eat now, pay later" partnership was mocked widely as a debt meme (snippet). https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/doordash-klarna-buy-now-pay-later-food-debt-rcna197354
- **2023:** the "no tip, slower delivery" warning drew backlash (snippet). https://www.androidheadlines.com/2023/11/doordash-warning-customers-order-without-tip-take-longer-to-deliver.html
- Driver-pay criticism follows the brand into its comments. https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/03/06/doordash-duolingo-zaria-parvez-hire/

**Team**

## Step 3 — Synthesis

### Transferable rules

Each rule: what to do → which brand proves it → how a small team does it cheaply. The "how" lines are inference; the proof lines are sourced above.

1. **Keep food and offers to a minority of the feed.** *Proof:* Zomato posts about 8% food, Swiggy about 32% (Qoruz 2026). *Cheaply:* of four weekly posts, at most one or two are a dish or an offer; the rest are entertainment, town life, the mascot, or a data line.

2. **Apply the group-chat test before approving.** *Proof:* DoorDash: "good enough to share in a group chat if you didn't work at DoorDash". *Cheaply:* the human approver asks one question: "would I forward this to my family group?" If not, it is a notice, not a post.

3. **One good sentence beats production.** *Proof:* Zomato's best-remembered posts are single lines in everyday speech ("eat home food sometimes"; "stop sending food to your ex on cash on delivery"). *Cheaply:* a one-line post in local dialect on a plain brand-colour card; no shoot, no food image needed, so the real-photo rule is not touched.

4. **Sometimes say the opposite of selling.** *Proof:* Zomato's "eat home food too" was copied by other brands for days. *Cheaply:* one post a month that is on the customer's side against your own interest ("your mother's cooking wins today").

5. **Give the mascot a job, not a logo slot.** *Proof:* HungerStation's box solves small daily problems in short funny videos (+35.4% Ramadan orders, agency-reported); foodpanda's Pau-Pau best content does not sell at all. *Cheaply:* one weekly mascot scene with a small story (late for iftar, stuck in rain, guarding the bag) and no offer. The mascot carries a closed bag or box — never an AI-rendered dish.

6. **Let the mascot stand next to real food, never replace it.** *Proof:* Zomato states it uses no AI in marketing creative; Swiggy's AI cat-chef images are the opposite choice. *Cheaply:* composite the 3D courier over a real restaurant photo or real footage, or keep the food out of frame. (Inference from the two contrasting choices; the hard rule is yours.)

7. **Turn order data into jokes and numbers.** *Proof:* Instamart's "someone just ordered 51 coconuts", Swiggy's "158 biryanis a minute", Talabat's "surprise champion" mini pizza, Careem's two-minute Doritos delivery. *Cheaply:* one database query a month → "strangest order", "most ordered in <town>", "latest order of the week". Never name or identify a real customer.

8. **Publish two data stories a year.** *Proof:* How India Swiggy'd, Talabat's 26 December report, Careem's post-Ramadan trends. *Cheaply:* a five-slide carousel at year end and one after Ramadan, one quirky number per slide, with real photos of the winning dishes from the restaurants.

9. **Ramadan is for giving, couriers and safety — not jokes.** *Proof:* Talabat's Share an Iftar, Hero Meals and "Don't Rush, Stay Safe"; Careem's tip matching. *Cheaply:* one simple mechanic (add a courier's iftar or a tip), one "don't rush before iftar, we will get there" mascot post, and a results post at month end.

10. **Save the humour for Eid and ordinary days.** *Proof:* Careem's sheep puns for Eid al-Adha, made in-house, published about two weeks ahead. *Cheaply:* one pun post per Eid, planned two weeks before.

11. **Build on a local saying or belief.** *Proof:* HungerStation's Birthmark Stories used "wahma" and won a regional Grand Prix with +12.2% awareness. *Cheaply:* a monthly post built on a dialect proverb or a shared habit about food and hospitality; ask followers for their version in the comments.

12. **Ask followers to do something small and silly.** *Proof:* Swiggy's Voice of Hunger (150,000+ entries in 10 days), fan-theory contest, DM fill-in-the-blank (+125% engagement). *Cheaply:* a fill-in-the-blank or "guess the dish from the close-up" (a real photo) with a small order credit as the prize.

13. **Make giveaways order-based with automatic entry.** *Proof:* Talabat's back-to-school draw: three orders in a week = one entry, no form. *Cheaply:* a weekly draw among customers who ordered N times; announce the winner's first name and town only with their consent.

14. **Set a 24–48 hour rule for trends, and plan only a few.** *Proof:* Swiggy skips a trend it cannot answer in 24–48 hours; DoorDash plans 3–4 reactive moments a quarter. *Cheaply:* if the approver cannot look at it today, drop it. Keep one of the four weekly slots free for "something people said this week".

15. **Pre-agree a fast lane with the approver.** *Proof:* DoorDash's quick channel and its question "what needs to be true to make this happen". *Cheaply:* one chat thread with the approver; reactive drafts go there with a deadline, and a list of topics that never need discussion because they are always "no" (rule 20).

16. **Join an existing joke with a twist, do not repeat it.** *Proof:* iFood's 44.84M-view post cost no media; its CMO: "if I join the conversation as just one more, I'll disappear". *Cheaply:* only react when the mascot or the delivery angle adds something new to the joke.

17. **Start from the comments.** *Proof:* DoorDash's Valentine's idea came from comments; commenters are about 5% of engagement but drive the narrative. *Cheaply:* a weekly ten-minute read of comments and messages; one post idea must come from it.

18. **Use small local creators with a loose brief, several pieces each.** *Proof:* Swiggy picks small, non-metro, meme-famous creators; Talabat's hackathon used micro and nano creators with no detailed brief and judges on shares and saves; DoorDash gets 6–7 pieces per deal; one 400K creator beat hundreds of other assets. *Cheaply:* one local creator a month, paid in order credit plus a small fee, three pieces from one visit to a real restaurant — which also supplies real food footage.

19. **Set a public reply standard and close the loop.** *Proof:* Talabat Kuwait requires replies within 3 hours; Careem responds within one hour, resolves within three, then tells the customer what was fixed. *Cheaply:* reply to every complaint the same day, move details to private messages, return to the public comment with the outcome.

20. **Religion, politics and identity are where every documented takedown happened.** *Proof:* Zomato's Mahakal and Kachra ads, Swiggy's Holi billboard, Careem's election puns and bride billboard. *Cheaply:* a written "never" list; a second reader for any festival or identity joke; if wrong, remove within a day with a plain apology (Zomato's one-day rollback).

21. **Show the couriers with respect, grounded in their real day.** *Proof:* Talabat's rider meals, heat rest stops and tips report; Swiggy's film built on rider research. Failures: Zomato's celebrity ads (2021) and Instamart's "brocode" comment (2025). *Cheaply:* a monthly real courier story with consent — a real photo beside the 3D mascot. Never joke about speed, uniforms or pay.

22. **Tease before a reveal.** *Proof:* HungerStation's unbranded-box teasers, then the reveal and a sticker pack. *Cheaply:* for a new town, feature or mascot outfit — two teaser posts, then the reveal; a free WhatsApp sticker pack of the mascot.

23. **Prioritise Reels over still images.** *Proof:* in the Gulf data, images are the most-posted format but not the best performer, and the standout post was a Reel (Emplifi, Q1 2026). *Cheaply:* at least two of four weekly posts as short videos — mascot animation or real restaurant footage.

24. **Decide in advance how much negativity is acceptable.** *Proof:* DoorDash's rule: 10–15% negative is fine, 30–50% is not — delete and learn. *Cheaply:* the approver checks comments one hour and one day after a risky post.

25. **Stress-test a promotion before posting it.** *Proof:* Grubhub's 2022 free-lunch promo hit about 6,000 orders a minute and overwhelmed restaurants. https://komonews.com/news/nation-world/grubhub-free-lunch-promotion-ends-up-being-huge-mess-for-nyc-restaurants-food-delivery-new-york-city *Cheaply:* confirm with the stores and cap the offer before any giveaway post goes out.

### What does NOT transfer

- **Celebrity and Super Bowl-scale ideas.** DoorDash's "All The Ads", Uber Eats' Beckham teaser, Talabat's Khaby Lame, iFood's creators with 20M+ followers. These need big budgets and an existing mass audience.
- **Persona-driven "admin" humour at volume.** Swiggy's 897.8M views rest on daily posting to 1.5M followers who already know the character; at four posts a week the running joke builds too slowly (inference).
- **Banter with other big brands.** Zomato–Blinkit billboards and Coca-Cola commenting on Instamart work because both sides are famous.
- **Real-time war rooms.** iFood's 30-person World Cup room and Zomato's push notification an hour after a news event are impossible with a human approving every post — and the approval step is worth more than the speed (inference).
- **A logo-free ad.** Zomato's unbranded birthday page works only after 18 years of recognition.
- **"Go viral every 30 days" as a goal.** DoorDash sets it with a team of 8 plus agencies.
- **AI-generated food or characters cooking food.** Swiggy's AI cat chef — ruled out by your own rule.
- **Edgy or adult jokes.** DoorDash's halftime post went out without pre-approval to a US audience; it does not fit a family audience in an Arabic market (inference).
- **Sponsor-level football and TV tie-ins.** Talabat's streaming perk, iFood's reality-show sponsorship. A small brand can post about a match; it cannot buy the association.
- **Engagement-rate targets copied from these accounts.** The benchmark figures found (1.19%–2.36% for the Indian accounts, a median of 20 interactions per Reel for Gulf e-commerce) come from different tools and markets; use your own last three months as the baseline (inference).

### Gaps in this study

- Posting cadence, format mix, Reel length and caption habits are not documented in any open source for any brand. The only way to get them is to look at the accounts by hand while logged in.
- Jahez and HungerStation's everyday tone, and Talabat's per-country accounts and Egypt humour, could not be verified.
- Most campaign numbers are self-reported by the brand or its agency.
